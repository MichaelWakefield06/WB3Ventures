Common questions

What's the monthly fee for?

Hosting, SSL, and ongoing updates, plus SEO work on the Standard and Pro tiers. It keeps your site fast, secure, and current instead of going stale the day it launches.

Who owns my website and domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and the site is yours. You're never locked in.

How long does it take?

Often just a few days, depending on the size of the build. I only take on a handful of projects at a time so each one gets real attention.

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay. I build the site first so you can judge the real thing, not a mockup. It only goes live once you're happy.

Do you work with businesses outside Greenville?

Yes. I'm based in the Upstate and focus on Greenville and nearby towns, but the work is done online, so location isn't a barrier.
