Local SEO & Google Business Profile in Greenville, SC.

Get your business showing up in the map pack when nearby customers search, so the people already looking for what you do actually find you.

When someone in Greenville searches for what you do, Google shows three businesses in a map at the top before anything else. Those three get the calls. If you're not one of them, most people never scroll far enough to find you.

Local SEO is the work of getting you into that group. It's part your website and part your Google Business Profile, and it's the difference between having a website and having one that actually brings in customers.

What I set up for you

  • Google Business Profile, built or cleaned up
  • Local keywords for Greenville and nearby towns
  • Consistent name, address, and phone everywhere
  • Directory listings and citations
  • On-page SEO tied to your service area
  • A simple plan for getting more reviews

The honest part

Nobody can guarantee a number one spot on Google, and anyone who promises it is selling you something that isn't true. Rankings come from real work over time: a solid site, a well-kept Google profile, genuine reviews, and links from other sites. What I do is put every piece you control in place and keep it maintained, so you're actually in the running instead of invisible. That's the difference between a fair shot and no shot.

Why local matters more than national

You don't need to beat the whole internet. You need to beat the other Greenville businesses a customer three miles away is choosing between. That's a fight a small business can win, and it's exactly where I aim your site.
